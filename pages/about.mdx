Most weeks split between two very different kinds of work: a storefront business that needs a site built to convert a Saturday-morning visitor, and a public agency or contractor that needs a site built to survive an RFP review and a resident who's never filed a form online before.

Neither brief is harder than the other — they're just optimizing for different things, and the discipline of figuring out which is the actual first step on every project.

The design system behind every project — the tokens, the component library, the motion budget — is the same one built out publicly in this site's own [Work](/work) section and design-system demos. What changes per project is how much of that budget gets spent, and where.

Based in California's Central Valley, working with clients locally and remotely. See [Services](/services) for what's on offer, or [get in touch](/contact) directly.
