Mid Cal Industrial Properties has 42 years of brokerage history in the Central Valley's industrial real-estate market, but the site representing that history hadn't kept pace with it.

The rebuild prototype is a straightforward listings-and-credibility site: property inventory up front, decades of track record made legible instead of implied, built mobile-first since a meaningful share of inquiries start from a phone on a job site.

Prototype built in Next.js, live for review at donovanuy.com/prototypes/midcalprop.
