Valley Portrait Co. shoots professional headshots in Stockton, CA — a business where the entire funnel is "convince someone to book a 20-minute session," which the previous site made harder than it needed to be.

The rebuild leads with portfolio work and a direct path to booking on every page, with pricing presented plainly instead of requiring an inquiry just to see a number.

Built as a prototype rebuild ahead of a client proposal, using the same component discipline as every other studio project — real content, no lorem ipsum, so what a client reviews is what they'd actually get.
