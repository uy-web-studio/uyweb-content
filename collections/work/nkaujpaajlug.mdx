Janell Nkaujpaajlug Yang needed a single place to send sponsors and family during her pageant run — somewhere to explain who she is, show her portfolio, and make it simple to contribute, without asking anyone to navigate a generic donation platform.

The site is built around five pages — Home, About, Sponsor Janell, Portfolio, and Contact — with the sponsorship flow as the clear priority action on every page rather than buried in a menu.

It's a smaller build than the studio's agency work, but the brief is the same in miniature: figure out the one thing a visitor needs to do, then get everything else out of its way.
