Janell Nkaujpaajlug Yang needed a dedicated hub to direct sponsors and family during her pageant run. She needed somewhere to introduce herself, showcase her portfolio, and accept contributions without routing supporters through a cluttered third-party fundraising platform.

The site features five core pages (Home, About, Sponsor Janell, Portfolio, and Contact), placing the sponsorship flow as the primary action on every screen rather than burying it inside navigation menus.

While smaller in scope than agency client contracts, the guiding principle remained identical: identify the primary action a visitor took to get here, and remove every distraction from their path.
